Privacy Policy

Effective and last updated: September 17, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how the developer of I Love Kanji ("I Love Kanji," "we," "us," or "our") handles information in connection with the I Love Kanji mobile application (the "App").

Privacy at a glance

I Love Kanji does not require an in-app account and does not use an advertising SDK. Learning progress, saved stories, review state, streaks, settings, and the local purchase receipt are stored on your device. The App uses Google Firebase Analytics for usage analytics and Google Play Billing for the optional one-time N4–N1 unlock. The App does not intentionally send the text of your personal kanji stories to Firebase Analytics, and we do not sell your personal information.

1. Scope

This Privacy Policy applies to information handled through the App. It does not control the independent privacy practices of Google, device manufacturers, app stores, operating systems, or other third-party services that may be used in connection with the App.

3. Local learning data and stories

The App is designed to store core learning information locally on your device. This may include your kanji progress, review history, saved stories, streak state, preferences, and similar learning-related settings.

We do not intentionally transmit the text of your personal kanji stories to Firebase Analytics. If the App's architecture changes in the future so that such content is transmitted to a server or cloud service, this Privacy Policy will be updated accordingly.

4. Firebase Analytics

The App may use Google Firebase Analytics to measure general use of the App and help improve stability, usability, and features. Firebase Analytics may automatically process technical and usage information such as app interactions, session information, device or app characteristics, and analytics identifiers.

We use analytics for legitimate product purposes such as understanding feature usage, identifying errors or friction, and improving the App. The current App logs event names for items such as level starts/completions, selected N5 learning milestones, notification permission results, whether a suggested or personal story was used (without sending the story text), story skips, and purchase-flow status. The App code does not set your email address, name, or a custom I Love Kanji account identifier as a Firebase Analytics user ID.

Firebase Analytics also automatically logs certain events and technical information, such as first open, sessions, engagement, app/device information, analytics installation identifiers, and supported Google Play purchase events, subject to Google's SDK behavior and configuration.

Firebase and Google process information under their own terms and privacy documentation. You can learn more from Firebase Privacy and Security and the Google Privacy Policy.

5. Purchases and Google Play Billing

The App may offer a one-time paid unlock for additional learning content through Google Play Billing. Google Play processes the payment transaction. We do not receive or store your complete payment-card number, bank-account credentials, or other full payment credentials.

Google Play may make purchase-status or entitlement information available to the App so that paid access can be granted or restored. Google's processing of payment and account information is governed by Google's own terms and privacy policies.

6. Notifications and device features

The App may request Android notification permission so it can schedule local learning reminders while the day's required learning remains incomplete. The reminder system can also respond to device restart, time changes, timezone changes, and app updates so local reminders can be restored or adjusted. These reminders are generated on the device rather than through an I Love Kanji cloud-messaging account.

You can disable notifications through your device settings. The App also uses device vibration for learning feedback, including incorrect-answer and completion feedback. These device features do not require the App to access your contacts, microphone, camera, messages, or precise location.

7. Data we do not intentionally request for normal App use

The App does not require you to create an I Love Kanji account and does not intentionally request access to your contacts, call logs, SMS messages, microphone, camera, or precise location for its normal kanji-learning functionality.

8. How information is used

Information described in this Policy may be used to:

  • provide lessons, reviews, streaks, progress tracking, audio, and related App functionality;
  • remember your settings and learning state on your device;
  • process or restore purchased access through Google Play;
  • measure App usage and improve performance, usability, and features;
  • detect, investigate, or address technical problems, abuse, or security issues; and
  • comply with applicable legal obligations when required.

9. Sharing and disclosure

We do not sell your personal information. The audited final project does not contain an in-app advertising SDK and does not operate an I Love Kanji account or cloud-sync service. Information may nevertheless be processed by third-party platform services necessary to operate the App, including Google Firebase Analytics, Google Play Billing, and FormSubmit when you choose to use the website contact form.

Information may also be disclosed if reasonably necessary to comply with applicable law, lawful process, or enforceable governmental requests, or to protect legal rights, safety, security, or the integrity of the App.

10. Data retention and deletion

Local App data generally remains on your device until you clear the App's storage, reset relevant App data, or uninstall the App, subject to your operating system and any device-level backup or restore features.

Data processed by third-party services such as Firebase Analytics or Google Play is retained according to those providers' applicable settings, retention periods, and policies.

Because I Love Kanji does not require its own user account, there is no separate I Love Kanji account-deletion process. To remove locally stored learning data, you can clear the App's storage or uninstall the App. For privacy questions, contact us at the email listed below.
